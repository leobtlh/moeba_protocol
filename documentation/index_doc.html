Introduction & Soft Default

Moeba Protocol is the first decentralized parametric insurance infrastructure introducing the "Soft Default" mechanism to democratize Catastrophe Bonds (Cat Bonds) via EVM compatible networks.

The Binary Risk Problem

Traditional Cat Bonds are inaccessible due to binary risk: if a hurricane hits 250km/h, investors lose 100% of their capital. If it's 249km/h, they lose nothing. This "Russian roulette" profile keeps DeFi liquidity away from real-world reinsurance yields.

The Moeba Solution

We transform binary risk into a manageable, linear risk via the Hybrid Parametric Insurance Vault (HPIV). Through sequential loss absorption, an extreme event results in a partial loss (e.g., 14%) for Senior investors, rather than total annihilation.

Economics

The 4-Step Waterfall

The cornerstone of the protocol is its capital hierarchy. Losses are absorbed sequentially to protect the Senior Tranche.

1. Insurer First Loss Capital

The Sponsor (Insurer) must deploy their own capital before opening the vault. This acts as the primary shield absorbing 100% of initial losses.

2. Premium Reserve Buffer

The upfront insurance premium paid by the Sponsor is ring-fenced. It acts as a secondary buffer against claims.

3. Junior Tranche (ERC-20)

High-risk DeFi investors who absorb the remaining losses in exchange for leveraged, highly boosted APR.

4. Senior Tranche (ERC-4626)

The protected core. These investors are only exposed if the catastrophe completely annihilates the first three layers.

IoT & Smart Twistlocks

For physical assets (Real Estate and Maritime), the protocol integrates directly with IoT sensor data to trigger claims instantly without human loss adjusters.

Maritime Logistics

A critical use case is marine container loss. By utilizing Smart Twistlocks (the securing mechanisms on cargo ships equipped with connection sensors), the Oracle can precisely detect how many containers have fallen overboard based on sudden signal loss.

Example Payout Logic:

Trigger: 15 units (Smart Twistlock signal lost)
Agreed Payout: $10,000 per container
Total Claim: $150,000 processed instantly via Oracle.

UMA Optimistic Oracle

Moeba Protocol utilizes the UMA Optimistic Oracle (OO) to securely bring real-world data on-chain. Rather than relying on rigid, pre-programmed price feeds, UMA allows any verifiable truth to be asserted on-chain.

  • 1. Assertion: A bot or user asserts that a specific event occurred (e.g., "Hurricane Milton reached Category 4").
  • 2. Dispute Window: A liveness period (e.g., 2 hours) opens. If no one disputes the claim, it is taken as truth.
  • 3. DVM Resolution: If disputed, UMA token holders vote to resolve the truth via the Data Verification Mechanism.
Legal

VUSA & DLT Law (Swiss)

Moeba operates under the cutting-edge Swiss regulatory framework, bridging DeFi and institutional compliance.

VUSA (ISPV) Structure

Under the revised Swiss Insurance Supervision Act (ISA Art. 30e), Moeba acts as an Insurance Special Purpose Vehicle. Because vaults are 100% "Fully Funded" upfront, capital requirements are drastically reduced, eliminating counterparty risk.

DLT Law & Ledger-based Securities

HPIV tokens are not mere utility tokens. Under Art. 973d of the Swiss Code of Obligations, they are classified as Ledger-based Securities (Droits-Valeurs Inscrits). Transferring the token legally transfers the underlying debt claim.

Governance & Security

To avoid the pitfalls of "1-token-1-vote" governance (whale centralization), Moeba utilizes a Tokenless Reputation Council.

The 3-of-5 Multisig

The `DAO_ROLE` is held by a Gnosis Safe controlled by 5 distinct seats, requiring 3 signatures for any upgrade:

  • Core Team (Technical updates)
  • Insurer Seat (Sponsor representative)
  • Investor Seat (DeFi liquidity provider)
  • Security Seat (Independent Auditor)
  • Legal Seat (Swiss compliance expert)

Timelock & Fork-free Exit

Every protocol upgrade is subject to a strict 48-hour Timelock. This guarantees the ultimate decentralization right: the ability for investors to withdraw their funds before any malicious update is applied.
